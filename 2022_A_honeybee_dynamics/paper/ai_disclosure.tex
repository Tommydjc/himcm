\noindent
This report documents artificial-intelligence assistance used while
preparing our contest materials, in accordance with COMAP contest rules
on AI disclosure. Tooling was limited to the Cursor IDE agent for code
scaffolding and LaTeX formatting support. No AI system was used to invent
numerical results; all tables and figures are produced by executing our
Python pipeline and reading \texttt{results/*.csv}.

\section{What the human team did independently}
We explicitly attest that the following were conceived, specified, and
signed off by the student team without outsourcing scientific judgment
to an AI system:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item \textbf{Differential-equation mechanism:} choice of the five
        compartments \(B,H,F,P,N\), dual-feedback structure (social
        inhibition and nutritional gating), and stress-channel meanings
        (pesticide, Varroa, frost).
  \item \textbf{Activation-function smoothing design:} decision to use
        Holling-III, Softplus state projection, and seasonal
        \(\Omega(t)\) so that Autograd elasticities are well-defined.
  \item \textbf{Summary Sheet:} narrative priorities and the final
        one-page claims; every numeric entry was typed from measured CSV
        outputs after human inspection.
  \item \textbf{Grower / association advisory letter:} field
        recommendations on fan placement, bloom feeding, and Varroa
        triggers were written as a human policy brief; AI only helped
        with LaTeX layout.
\end{enumerate}

\section{What AI assistance was used for}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item Drafting boilerplate for PyTorch \texttt{nn.Module} parameter
        registration and day-loop bookkeeping.
  \item Generating initial LaTeX \texttt{booktabs} tables and
        \texttt{includegraphics} paths tied to existing figure filenames.
  \item Fixing pytest \texttt{pythonpath} so \texttt{from src...}
        imports resolve.
\end{itemize}
AI suggestions were reviewed, edited, and re-run by the team. When an
AI-proposed number disagreed with a CSV, the CSV prevailed.
