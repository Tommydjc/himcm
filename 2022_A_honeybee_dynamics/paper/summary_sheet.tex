\noindent\textbf{What we built.}
A five-state dual-feedback DFM colony ODE
\(y=[B,H,F,P,N]\) with \(C^\infty\) activations, unrolled in PyTorch for
Autograd elasticities; a triple-stress CCD bifurcation in forager
mortality \(\mu_F\); and a 20-acre almond competition--yield--rent OR
model. All figures in \texttt{paper\_figures/} are generated from code;
no hand-edited CSV cells.

\noindent\textbf{Req 3 --- 20-acre almond stocking
(\texttt{orchard\_pollination\_optimization.csv}).}
Golden band \textbf{1--2 hives/acre} \(\Leftrightarrow\)
\textbf{\(K\in[20,40]\)} colonies on 20 acres.
Band optimum \(K^\star=40\):
\(\mathrm{YieldRatio}=\mathbf{0.848}\) (\(84.8\%\) of full set),
net profit \$51{,}394, hive-health index \(0.975\).
At \(K=35\) (1.75/acre): yield \(0.831\), net \$51{,}136.
Overstocking to \(K=60\) keeps yield \(\approx 0.855\) but drops health
to \(0.735\) and net to \$47{,}826.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Req 4 --- Grower takeaway.}
Protect queen laying and keep effective forager death below the soft tip
under combined stress; stock almonds in the \(20\)--\(40\) colony band;
see \texttt{grower\_advisory\_letter.tex} for placement, feeding, and
Varroa triggers.
