\noindent\textbf{What we built.}
Hay--Davies PV and Markov load (8760\,h). SOC/SOH pack physics.
SciPy HiGHS MILP for \(x_k\in\{0..4\}\) and typical-day EMS. Budget

hand-edited CSV cells.

\medskip

(\texttt{annual\_pv\_load\_8760h.csv}).}
PV \(\mathbf{12{,}266\,kWh/y}\) (mean \(1.400\,\mathrm{kW}\)).
Load \(\mathbf{15{,}398\,kWh/y}\) (mean \(1.758\,\mathrm{kW}\)).

\(\approx 20\%\) with this array, with or without packs.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Tasks 2--3 --- \$10{,}000 BOM
(\texttt{optimal\_sizing\_solution.csv}).}
\textbf{3 FREEDOH packs + 1 lead-carbon pack}
(\(24.6\,\mathrm{kWh}\), \(10.5\,\mathrm{kW}\), capex \textbf{\$9{,}700}).
Tesla Powerwall+ costs \$10{,}500. It is \textbf{not} in this mix.
It appears only at the \$18k Pareto-front row.
8760-hour LPSP \(\mathbf{0.319}\); outage hours \(\mathbf{3{,}057}\);
outage events \(\mathbf{366}\); 10-year TCO \$157{,}669.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Budget knees
(\texttt{budget\_pareto\_front.csv}).}
We mark \$8{,}000 and \$10{,}000 on the Pareto-front figure.
\$8k BOM: 2 FREEDOH + 1 lead-carbon, \textbf{3{,}576\,h / 391 events},
LPSP \(0.362\).
\$10k: as above, \textbf{3{,}057\,h / 366 events}.
Events are contiguous deficit runs (hundreds per year). They are
\emph{not} ``six outages.'' \$12k--\$15k is a hardware plateau (same BOM,
2{,}810\,h).

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Task 5 --- Homeowner takeaway.}
Do not buy panels as a winter-pack substitute. Size storage from usable
kWh and DoD, not nameplate slogans. In dark-cold hours, defer
dryer/dishwasher, dim lights, and keep fridge, well, router, and the
work laptop. See \texttt{letter\_to\_homeowners.tex}.
