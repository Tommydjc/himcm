\noindent\textbf{Background.}
TOU electricity tariff peaks punish reactive cooling.
Bang-Bang hysteresis short-cycles the compressor.
We build AeroTherm-MPC on a 2R2C thermal network.
Wall mass stores pre-cooling before peak rates.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Model and algorithm.}
Envelope capacitances are $C_{\mathrm{air}}=6.2\times 10^5$ and
$C_{\mathrm{wall}}=1.8\times 10^7\,\mathrm{J/^\circ C}$.
COP is $3.8-0.04(T_{\mathrm{out}}-25)$.
Comfort uses ISO 7730 PMV with penalty outside $|\mathrm{PMV}|>0.5$.
TOU rates are $\$0.12/\$0.22/\$0.48$ (peak $14{:}00$--$19{:}00$).
Zero-order hold at $T_s=15\,\mathrm{min}$ yields prediction matrices.
Model predictive control (MPC) uses horizon $H=24$.
SciPy L-BFGS-B solves each window.
Cognitive agents may retune the comfort band via Ollama or Mock.
