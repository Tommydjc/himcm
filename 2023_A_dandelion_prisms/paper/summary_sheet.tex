\noindent\textbf{What we built.}
A vectorized pappus kernel (Gaussian near field + WALD tail) is convolved
on a $100\times 100$ m lawn. Weekly west-edge Lefkovitch answers official
Req~1. A daily center-plant CA is a second geometry---never averaged into
the weekly cover. Fisher--KPP supplies $c^{\ast}=2\sqrt{rD}$ with
$D=M_2/(4\tau)$. Official Req~2 is a three-species SAW. Mowing Pareto and
the HOA letter are course pages.
