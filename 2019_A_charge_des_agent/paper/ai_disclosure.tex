\section{Local generation of simulation data}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item All DES KPIs were produced on a local Python $3.10+$ environment
        (SimPy + NumPy/Pandas/Matplotlib).
  \item FastMCP tools call the same in-process DES functions used by
        scripts; they do not fetch external contest data.
  \item Ollama runs locally as \texttt{qwen2.5:7b}.
        If Ollama is offline, a deterministic rule proposer continues.
        The DES engine still executes every candidate pack.
  \item Locked evidence includes
        \texttt{stage2\_baseline\_metrics.csv},
        \texttt{optimal\_kiosk\_sizing.csv},
        \texttt{thunderstorm\_stress\_comparison.csv},
        and the $300$\,DPI figures under \texttt{paper\_figures/}.
\end{enumerate}

\section{What the human team did independently}
We attest that the following were conceived, specified, and signed off
by the student team:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item \textbf{Scientific design:} BDI passenger agent states, SimPy
        resource pools, CapEx/area caps, storm injection rules, and
        Pareto objectives.
  \item \textbf{Acceptance criteria:} mean wait $\le 5$ minutes and
        reneging probability $\le 0.05$.
  \item \textbf{Summary Sheet:} narrative priorities and final one-page
        claims.
        Every numeric entry was typed from measured CSV outputs after
        human inspection.
        The Summary Sheet was completed independently by human teammates.
  \item \textbf{Airport authority letter:} operational SOP language
        (three-minute walk grid; free first $15$ minutes on the fast
        kiosk; storm lane checklist) was completed independently by
        human teammates.
        AI assistance, if any, was limited to LaTeX layout only.
\end{enumerate}

\section{What AI assistance was used for}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item Drafting SimPy process boilerplate and FastMCP tool signatures.
  \item Driving LangGraph reflection / planning loops via local Ollama.
  \item Generating initial LaTeX table/figure wiring tied to existing
        CSV and PNG filenames.
  \item Fixing pytest paths and small typing/docstring gaps.
\end{itemize}

\section{What AI was not used for}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item Inventing or editing locked KPI cells in \texttt{results/}.
  \item Fabricating storm or Pareto statistics for the Summary Sheet.
  \item Submitting without human review of every reported number.
\end{itemize}
