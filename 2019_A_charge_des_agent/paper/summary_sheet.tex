\noindent\textbf{Problem.}
Size terminal charging kiosks under CapEx $\le\$80{,}000$ and area
$\le 40\,\mathrm{m}^2$.
Each traveler is a BDI passenger agent with SOC and boarding slack.
Agents choose a fast kiosk, a slow locker, a power bank, or abort.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Model.}
SimPy maps three resource pools: $65$\,W fast kiosk, $15$\,W locker,
power-bank bank.
Arrivals use a $16$-hour NHPP with three peaks.
LangGraph + local Ollama (\texttt{qwen2.5:7b}) propose packs.
FastMCP tools call the DES and check budget.
Acceptance: mean wait $\le 5$ min; reneging probability $\le 0.05$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Stage-2 baseline ($12/15/2$, normal day).}
$n=441$ arrivals; $110$ anxious seekers.
Mean wait $=0.00$ min.
Reneging probability $=0.00$.
Fast utilization $=5.72\%$; slow utilization $=24.24\%$.
Max system load $=17$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Pareto sizing (rush densify; reneging $\le 5\%$).}
$216$ unique packs; $7$ non-dominated points.
Knee under the $\$80{,}000$ line: pack $\mathbf{8/16/0}$.
CapEx $=\mathbf{\$35{,}200}$ ($44\%$ of CapEx cap).
Area $=38.4\,\mathrm{m}^2$.
Rush mean wait $=0.0115$ min; reneging probability $=0.00$.
Cheapest Pareto pack is $4/8/0$ (wait $5.91$ min).
Knee wait is $0.0115$ min.
Mean wait falls by $99.8\%$ between those packs.
